First-load screen in orange and blue

Round 2 uses two colours only: Claude orange and Codex blue, plus pale light where they meet. The art is made from ASCII characters, with shade standing in for depth. Each frame is a live terminal at the app's size (76 × 22). The counter is staged to loop for the board; in the app it counts real files.

Round 2, kept for comparison

Rules that apply to whichever one you pick

  • When it shows: while the first scan runs, and for at least about 1 s. Any key skips it, and q quits.
  • Honest counter: the numbers are real files read so far, never a fake progress bar.
  • Any terminal: it falls back to 256 colours (try the button) and needs nothing installed. With NO_COLOR set, it drops to plain shade characters.
  • Hand-over: when loading ends the art fades out and the home screen appears, with no extra wait.
  • Small windows: below 60 × 16 the art shrinks to fit and the counter stays.
