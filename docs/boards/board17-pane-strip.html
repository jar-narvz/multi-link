Board 17 · step strip, actions and notifications

These build on the pane you approved in Phase 16 (board 16 C). There are three parts: a strip for this session's PLAN.md step, row actions, and a notification when a Codex hand-off finishes. Pick one option in each part. The rows are example data in the shape of real pane and graph output.

1 · Step strip

It sits just under the header and shows how this session's step is going across both tools. It reuses the TUI step graph's parts (plan → implement → review).

S1Chain

One line per part, in order: who did it, what kind of work it was, and its status. It reads like the TUI's step graph, folded down to fit the pane.

2 · Row actions

Clicking a row selects it and shows what's known about it, with buttons. Codex sessions can't be opened automatically (a limit already stated in the README), so Codex rows only get Copy id.

A1Expand in place

The clicked row grows two detail lines and its buttons. Clicking it again folds it back.
